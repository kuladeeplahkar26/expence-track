# Business Spreadsheets – Expense Tracker
**Executive Gold & Obsidian Business Expenditure Management Dashboard**

---

## 1. Executive Summary
**Business Spreadsheets - Expense Tracker** is an ultra-premium executive financial suite designed for high-growth businesses and enterprise finance managers. Replicating the gold and black luxury visual hierarchy from the reference design, it combines professional expense logging, category distribution, time-series spending analysis, payment method ratios, and ranked top expenses into a high-performance, responsive single-page web application.

## 3. Data Integrity & Real-Time Intelligence
- **Clean Slate Initialization**: Starts with zero demo data so users have a 100% clean slate immediately upon launch.
- **Dynamic Real-Time Calculations**: All metrics, charts, budget gauges, and executive insights are computed live from user transactions.
- **Client-Side Privacy**: 100% private, in-browser persistence.

---

## 4. Technical Architecture
- **HTML5 (`index.html`)**: Semantic executive structure with Cinzel, Playfair Display, Plus Jakarta Sans, and JetBrains Mono typography.
- **CSS3 (`style.css`)**: Obsidian dark surfaces (`#050505` to `#18181c`), metallic gold borders (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.32)`), and gold gradient accents.
- **JavaScript ES6+ (`script.js`)**: Dynamic state calculations, Chart.js multi-chart instances, modal lifecycle, and CSV export.
- **Live Local Server**: Active at `http://localhost:8080/`.
